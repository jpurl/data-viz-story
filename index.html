Justin D. Purl

Head of People Systems & Analytics @ Archer

I am an Industrial/Organizational Psychologist (Ph.D. ABD) with a passion for blending human behavior, statistics, and technology.

Over the past decade, I have led teams at Google, TikTok, Zscaler, and Gilead Sciences, transforming how organizations approach people analytics. My expertise lies in designing HRIS architectures, developing custom AI agents, and applying advanced psychometrics and survival analysis to turn complex data into actionable strategies for talent acquisition and retention.
