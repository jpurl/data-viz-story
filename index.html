People keep asking me what "secret tool" I use to make my data visualizations stand out.

Tableau?

PowerBI?

Looker?

Matplotlib?

Nope.

I’m going to share a slightly controversial opinion...

If you want your data to look incredible in a web browser, you should probably use the languages that were built specifically for the web browser.

For 30 years, the smartest engineers on the planet have been ruthlessly optimizing HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

So why do we spend hours wrestling with clunky drag-and-drop BI tools trying to make them look like modern web apps?

When you code your visualizations directly, everything changes. Here is what you unlock:

  • 🎨 Pixel-Perfect Control: No locked color palettes.
  • ⚡️ Limitless Interactivity: If you can dream it, you can build it.
  • 📱 True Responsiveness: Visuals that scale to mobile beautifully.
  • 🔗 Seamless Integration: Native to your app, no 8-second iframe loads.

The day I switched to coding natively for the browser was the day my work stopped being just "charts"...

And started being experiences.

Yes, the learning curve is steeper. But the ceiling is infinitely higher.
